# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- Four-mode graph workbench for exploration, algorithms, analysis, and editing.
- Animated BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, Kruskal, and greedy-coloring playback.
- Shortest-path highlighting and label-propagation community detection.
- Diameter, radius, center, girth, clustering coefficient, articulation points, bridges, Eulerian and Hamiltonian checks, bipartition coloring, exact planarity, degree distribution, and five centrality measures.
- Direct canvas vertex creation and dragging, click-to-connect editing, vertex renaming, element deletion, and weighted edges.
- Random regular graph generation.
- Topology locking for repeatable scatter-layout experiments.
- PNG visualization export.
- Persistent appearance presets and custom vertex, edge, selection, background, pattern, and label colors.
- Canvas pattern, label visibility, vertex scale, edge thickness, and ambient glow controls.

### Changed

- Scatter arrangements now receive a fresh layout seed without losing reproducible topology.
